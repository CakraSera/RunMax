Hard questions, one-breath answers

QuestionAnswer
Why only one hard day? One Quality day is the training signal; more is injury risk. The teacher (checkWeek) blocks extra ones — the model cannot ship them.
What is MCP? A standard door between the agent and tools. Our door: notes — searchNotes + readNote over 6 markdown files.
What is RAG here? The robot looks up coach notes before drafting instead of guessing from memory.
What is Langfuse? A camera on every run: one trace per build, every tool call a span. We debug with the traceId, like console.log.
Is the model trusted? No. checkWeek fails closed inside saveWeek; an illegal week cannot be saved, whatever the model says.
Pain → what exactly? Flag pain: zero Quality, zero intervals, walk/rest/easy only, and no diagnosis sentences anywhere.
Why not a 16-week calendar? Scope: one week, rebuilt over the same Monday. No history pile (ADR 0011). Simple to trust, simple to show.
