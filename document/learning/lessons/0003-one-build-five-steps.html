One build, five steps

The assembly line your robot runs every single time — and what to say out loud while each step happens in the demo.

First, remember (no peeking)

From lesson 0002: the diary is the Log, the plan is this week's 7 day-cards, the teacher is checkWeek, the camera is Langfuse.

The assembly line

Every build is the same five steps, in the same order, exactly once each (PRD §7):

#Real nameKid versionIn the demo, say…
1parseLogRead the diary“It reads the messy diary and pulls out clues — pain, walking, minutes.”
2retrieveNotesVisit the library“Before inventing anything, it borrows 6 coach notes — looks up, doesn’t make up.”
3draftWeekDraw the plan“Now it draws 7 day-cards, Monday to Sunday.”
4checkWeekTeacher checks“The teacher counts hard days and rest days. Illegal? Blocked — the robot fixes and redraws.”
5saveWeekPut it on the fridge“Only a legal plan gets saved. The model cannot cheat this step.”

One build = one film in the camera (one Langfuse trace). Each step shows up in the film — that is how you prove the order when someone asks.

Do it now (2 minutes)

  1. Run: pnpm --filter @runmax/agent runner:dev "Rabu lutut agak nyeri, jalan aja"
  2. While it runs, narrate steps 1–5 out loud, kid version.
  3. When it prints the Week: point at the output — “see? no hard day, because knee.”
  4. Copy the traceId if your teacher asked for tracing proof (lesson 0001).

Check yourself

1. Diary says knee pain. How many hard days may the plan have?

2. What does the teacher do with an illegal plan?

3. Which step visits the six coach notes?
