Lesson 0002 · Present RunMax

Your agent, like you're 7

One story, five sentences, four toys. Say it out loud twice and you own it.

The story in 5 sentences

  1. A runner keeps a messy diary (the Log) — sometimes Indonesian, sometimes English, sometimes both.
  2. A robot helper reads the diary and builds a plan for this week only: Monday to Sunday, 7 day-cards (the Board).
  3. The robot has rules: max 1 hard day, at least 1 rest day, and if the diary says "knee hurts" — zero hard days, and it never plays doctor.
  4. A teacher checks the plan before it ships (checkWeek). Illegal plan? Blocked. The robot retries until legal.
  5. A camera films everything (Langfuse): every thought, every tool, one film per build.

The 8 tools (robot's hands)

Weeksmith: parseLog · retrieveNotes · draftWeek · checkWeek · saveWeek. ConsultSmith: askCues · saveLog · buildThisWeek.

Kid version: read the diary → peek at the coach's notes → draw the plan → teacher check → ship it. The asking robot: re-read the diary → save diary → call the builder.

The library (MCP)

Before drawing the plan, the robot visits a tiny library of 6 coach notes (MCP = a door the robot knocks on to borrow books: searchNotes, readNote). It's RAG: look it up instead of make it up.

Report card (evals)

13 tests, all passing: 10 for the builder (empty diary, knee pain, two hard days, overwrite last build…), 3 for the asker (pain logged, asks once, never diagnoses). Kid version: a quiz the robot must pass every time we change it.
